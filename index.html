№ 01

About

Six-plus years of production machine learning, and the research that keeps coming out of it.

Barcelona, Spain

Six-plus years building and deploying large-scale ML systems, now at IQVIA in Barcelona. The site-tiering work is a family of ranking models that score and tier investigator sites so clinical-trial teams get an evidence-backed shortlist for study placement — plus the evaluation framework, orchestration and monitoring that decide when a new model is actually good enough to replace the one in production.

Before that, nearly three years at Clarivate on patent-domain NLP and generative AI: pretraining domain-specific language models, building taxonomy-guided classifiers, and productionising multi-agent prior-art research systems on AWS.

The work runs end to end — data pipelines, pretraining and fine-tuning in PyTorch and Spark/Databricks, and low-latency serving on AWS (SageMaker, Fargate, Bedrock).

Selected Experience

Seven positions, newest first — clinical trials, patents, search, speech and vision.

2020–present

  1. Jun 2026
    – Present

    IQVIA

    Senior Machine Learning Engineer

    Barcelona, Spain

    • Own the production ML system behind clinical-trial site tiering end to end — a family of ranking models (site participation, performance and delivery) plus the data pipeline that feeds them — used by clinical-trial teams to select investigator sites for studies.
    • Build and operate the automated model-evaluation framework: it discovers candidate models in MLflow, runs bulk predictions, and ranks each candidate against the current production model on versioned datasets and ranking metrics, making promote-or-hold an evidence-backed decision instead of a judgement call.
    • Guard the measurement itself: drift and feature-space-collapse detection against production, plausibility ceilings on candidate predictions, and de-duplication of identical retrains, so no model is promoted on a broken comparison.
    • Make model quality a live surface: Datadog dashboards defined as code — versioned in the repo and provisioned through Terraform, never hand-edited — fed by metric-push steps inside the evaluation pipeline, with alerting on regression, drift, and evaluation runs that report nothing at all.
    • Moved nightly data ingestion onto orchestrated Airflow DAGs with a real dependency graph, retries, data-quality gates and end-to-end failure alerting, replacing fragile cron-offset scheduling and cutting the nightly window from ~9 h to ~5 h.
    • Also operate a protocol-similarity retrieval service that surfaces the most similar prior trials, and drive documentation, dependency and vulnerability hygiene, and mentoring across the stack.
    • Model work is data-centric: improving the data a two-tower ranking network consumes rather than swapping architectures, with every change validated through the evaluation framework.
